Woodinville traditions

A few recurring Woodinville happenings are worth building a trip around even though they don't always publish a full year of dates in advance — bookmark these and check closer to the date.

💃

Ladies Nights

Wineries across town — Barnard Griffin's District Flats tasting room among them — run periodic Ladies Night pop-ups with live music, local vendors, and no cover charge.

Watch the Chamber calendar →
🍺

Woodinville Oktoberfest

An annual mid-to-late-September Bavarian celebration at Woodin Creek Village — specialty beers, live oompah bands, and proceeds benefiting Homeward Pet.

Check this year's date →
🎊

Parades & Festivals

The Celebrate Woodinville Parade & Festival runs each August (pancake breakfast, community parade down 175th, Basset Bash, and a Wilmot Gateway Park festival); a lighted car parade through Woodin Creek Village kicks off Winterfest each winter.
