Wineries with a street address in their listing are placed by that address, so zooming in shows who sits on which street and block (estimated from address numbers, not surveyed). Wineries with a dashed outline have no address listed yet and sit in a row at the bottom of their district. Downtown and Hollywood are on the east side of the Sammamish River Trail; the West Valley Industrial District is on the west side along the former East Railroad corridor.
